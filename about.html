The group团队简介

About us关于我们

We are a community of researchers at YMSC interested in geometric approaches to representation theory.

我们是丘成桐数学科学中心关注表示论几何方法的学者群体。

Our interests include:

  • Geometric representation theory
  • Algebraic and symplectic geometry
  • Categorification and representation theory
  • Geometric Langlands and related topics
